B.Tech EIE Curriculum Integration

Specialized Engineering Laboratories

Microprocessors & Embedded

8085/8086 Assembly, ARM Cortex, ESP32 Wi-Fi architecture, C++ firmware, and real-time operating systems (RTOS).

Process Control & PLC

Industrial ladder logic programming, Siemens S7 PLCs, Modbus RS485 telemetry, and SCADA monitoring dashboards.

Biomedical Instrumentation

Bio-signal acquisition (ECG, EMG, EEG), active op-amp filtering, isolation amplifiers, and LabVIEW GUI processing.

Sensors & Transducers

Precision RTDs, thermocouples, LVDTs, capacitive pressure sensors, and strain gauge signal conditioning circuits.
